# Title as a question

**Status:** historical | reviewed-not-executed | tested-on-synthetic-data | validated-against-original-results
**Script:** `script/NN-name.R`
**Test:** `tests/test-NN-name.R`
**Input data:** `data/file.csv`, described in `metadata/file.csv`
**Outputs:** `output/file.csv`

## Scientific question

What the analysis answers, in one or two sentences.

## Data and protocol

Where the data come from, the protocol that produced them, the sample, and any metadata the analysis depends on (observer, instrument, scale, timing, units).

## Method and why

The method, and the reason it fits the question and the data.

## Assumptions

Design and statistical assumptions the results rely on, and how you checked them.

## Alternatives

Other defensible methods, and when to prefer them.

## Diagnostics

Checks run before trusting the results, and what they showed.

## What the results support

Conclusions the results support, separated from interpretation and speculation.

## What the results do not support

Claims a reader might make that these results cannot back.

## Limitations

Known weaknesses and open questions.

## References

Primary sources for the data, the method and any claim above, with DOIs.
